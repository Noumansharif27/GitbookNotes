# RDP and SOCKS Tunneling with SocksOverRDP

* **Carregando SocksOverRDP.dll usando regsvr32.exe**

```sh
regsvr32.exe SocksOverRDP-Plugin.dll
```

* Podemos acesso remoto via RDP com o `mstsc.exe`
