# 3. Artificial Intelligence

This course introduces students to the basic knowledge representation, problem-solving, and learning methods of artificial intelligence. Upon completion of 6.034, students should be able to develop intelligent systems by assembling solutions to concrete computational problems; understand the role of knowledge representation, problem-solving, and learning in intelligent-system engineering; and appreciate the role of problem-solving, vision, and language in understanding human intelligence from a computational perspective. Learn more about AI by [reading these articles](http://laconicml.com/category/ai/).
