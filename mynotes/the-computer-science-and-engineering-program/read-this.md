# Read this

Most of the courses come from MIT. This program is based on free MIT courses that may help you become a Computer Science Engineer specializing in Data Science, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Natural Language Processing, and Computer Vision.

Since this is a complete Computer Science and Engineering curriculum, it will be organized into 8 semesters distributed over 4 academic years, though the time may be shorter.

Obviously, this is not meant to earn a bachelor’s degree — the goal is to gain equivalent knowledge.

It is intended for those who want a solid and comprehensive foundation in the fundamental concepts of all computing disciplines, and for those who have discipline, motivation, and (most importantly!) good habits to obtain this education largely on their own.
